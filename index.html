Urban Sandbox

v0.1 · A 3D sketchpad for architects & planners

Sketch building massing, lay out roads and plots, paint green spaces, analyze sunlight, and watch real-time urban metrics update as you design. Think of it as Lego — but scaled for real cities.

What You Can Do

  • 🧱Stack building blocks (8 sizes, 4 rotations)
  • 🛣️Paint roads with brush presets
  • 🌿Drop parks & rooftop gardens
  • ☀️Run sunlight shading analysis
  • 📐Auto-detect plots & compute FAR
  • 📊Choropleth density visualisation
  • 🔄Undo · Redo · Auto-save
  • 📱iPad & touchscreen ready

Quick Start

  1. Pick a canvas preset (60m → 640m) at the top
  2. Choose a mode: Road, Plot, or Build
  3. Left-click & drag to place. Right-click & drag to pan.
  4. Press R to rotate the current block.
Built with Three.js · Runs entirely in your browser
